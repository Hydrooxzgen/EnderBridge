# 贡献者公约 / Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## 我们的承诺 / Our Pledge

为了营造一个开放、友好的环境，我们作为贡献者和维护者承诺：无论年龄、体型、身体残障、民族、性别特征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济地位、国籍、个人外貌、种族、宗教或性取向如何，参与本项目的人都免受骚扰。

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## 我们的标准 / Our Standards

有助于营造积极环境的行为包括：

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- 使用友好、包容的语言 / Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- 尊重不同的观点和经验 / Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- 有建设性地接受并给予反馈 / Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- 为社区利益承担责任 / Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
- 关注对社区最有利的事情 / Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community

不可接受的行为包括：

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- 使用性化的语言或意象，以及性骚扰 / The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances
- 挑衅、侮辱或贬损性评论，以及人身攻击或政治攻击 / Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- 公开或私下的骚扰 / Public or private harassment
- 未经明确许可，发布他人的私人信息（如住址、邮箱）/ Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- 其他在专业环境中被合理认为不恰当的行为 / Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## 执行责任 / Enforcement Responsibilities

社区维护者有责任澄清和执行我们的行为标准，并会对任何他们认为不恰当、威胁、冒犯或有害的行为采取适当且公平的纠正措施。

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## 适用范围 / Scope

本行为准则适用于所有社区空间，也适用于个人在公共场合代表社区时的行为。

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## 执行 / Enforcement

如遇滥用、骚扰或其他不可接受的行为，请通过以下渠道举报：

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement via:

- **[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Hydrooxzgen/EnderBridge/issues)**（公开举报 / public reports）
- **[Discussions](https://github.com/Hydrooxzgen/EnderBridge/discussions)**（社区讨论 / community discussions）

所有举报都将被及时审查和调查，并得到必要且适当的回应。维护者有义务对举报者的身份保密。

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

## 执行指南 / Enforcement Guidelines

社区维护者将遵循以下社区影响指南来确定违反本行为准则的后果：

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. 纠正 / Correction

**社区影响**：使用不恰当的语言或社区中认为不专业或不受欢迎的其他行为。

**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.

**后果**：社区维护者发出书面警告，明确违规性质并解释行为为何不恰当。可要求公开道歉。

**Consequence**: A private, written warning from community leaders, providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. 警告 / Warning

**社区影响**：通过单次事件或一系列行为违规。

**Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**后果**：警告并说明持续行为的后果。在指定时间内不得与相关人员进行互动，包括主动与社区维护者互动。

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved for a specified period of time, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct.

### 3. 临时封禁 / Temporary Ban

**社区影响**：严重违反社区标准，包括持续不当行为。

**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**后果**：在指定时间内临时禁止与社区进行任何形式的互动或公开交流。

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period of time.

### 4. 永久封禁 / Permanent Ban

**社区影响**：表现出一种一贯的违规模式或骚扰个人或针对特定群体的敌意。

**Community Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**后果**：永久禁止在社区内进行任何形式的公共互动。

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community.
